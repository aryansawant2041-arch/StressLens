Three Models, Compared Fairly

Each trained and evaluated on the same real lifestyle data, so you can see exactly how baseline, bagging, and boosting compare.

🌳

Decision Tree

A single tree of yes/no questions on your lifestyle data. Simple, interpretable, and used here as the baseline model.

🌲

Random Forest

An ensemble of many decision trees (bagging) that vote together, reducing overfitting and revealing which lifestyle factors matter most.

⚡

Gradient Boosting

Trees built sequentially, each correcting the last one's mistakes (boosting) — often the most accurate of the three.
